Dear teacher,

This issue is about what happens when you give school students a real problem, a real mentor, and a real stage.

Studentpreneur is a programme run by the PIEDS Student Team at BITS Pilani. We bring in-person workshops on entrepreneurship to your campus, coach students through a 72-hour pitch competition, and end it all on a demo day in front of a jury.

Edition 1.0 ran at Birla Balika Vidyapeeth, Pilani. Edition 2.0 is opening to more schools. The pages that follow show what your students will do, and what you'll need to bring us in.

p. 04

workshop

We introduce students to entrepreneurship and startup fundamentals: key terminology, market analysis, why user feedback matters, product positioning, and case studies of startups that made it.

Keep scrolling, the pages turn sideways

1

Finance

Budgeting, cost analysis, pricing strategies, revenue models, fundraising, managing cash flow.

2

Ideation

Brainstorming creative solutions, identifying opportunities, refining ideas, developing viable startup concepts.

3

Validation

Testing ideas, gathering user feedback, analysing market fit, refining based on insights, ensuring product-market alignment.

4

Execution

Building prototypes, defining a roadmap, setting milestones, implementing solutions, testing scalability, refining processes.

p. 05

Activities

Between sessions we run engaging activities to keep the room lively and energetic, from the first hour to the last.

Founder's Wall

Students explore the traits of famous entrepreneurs and build a wall of inspiration.

Decode a failed startup

Students analyse why a real startup failed, then discover the actual reasons behind it.

Solve It

Students speak out problems they face every day and ideate solutions with their peers and us.

Logo Jam

A quick-fire challenge: sketch a logo for a made-up brand in 10 minutes.

Guess the Entrepreneur

A meme-based game to guess the entrepreneur or startup moment. Learn through laughter.

Idea to Pitch

72 hour competition

Students get 72 hours to build a pitch deck and a pitch around problem statements they identified during the workshop activities. Dedicated mentors guide each team, offer suggestions and help them through blockers.

0 hours in

Pick a problem

Teams choose a problem statement that came up in the workshop.
